Philadelphia · Health access · PublishedWhat happened to pharmacy access when Rite Aid left?Traces 41 closure locations from court filings and company records, then routes trips from 62 neighbourhoods to each former store and its designated receiving pharmacy: the average modelled walk rises from 9 to 16 minutes. Chicago · Food access · PublishedWho lost their grocery store when Walmart left Chicago?Verifies six closures and openings against federal retailer records, then routes trips from surrounding neighbourhoods: Kenwood shows the biggest modelled jump, from 11 to 16 minutes on foot. Philadelphia · R · CourseworkWhere do households live without a car?Spatial features from city open data cut cross-validated prediction error by about a quarter across 381 tracts.
